# Terminology evidence record

Keep one compact entry per concept and scope; reuse an existing format when it contains these fields.

- Original forms and occurrences, linked to manuscript locations.
- Concept, definition/formula, mathematical context and intended use.
- Adopted English or Japanese form and its scope.
- Status: attested usage, project editorial choice, no standard Japanese translation found after research, or research unresolved/inaccessible.
- Decision and rationale, including translation or English retention.
- External sources actually inspected, with exact edition/page/section and URL/DOI as applicable; identify which establish meaning and which attest Japanese usage. For a negative search, record the inspected sources and search scope.
- Permitted and rejected alternatives with their concept/use restrictions; do not describe a legitimate project-disfavoured alternative as mathematically incorrect.
- Correspondence to manuscript occurrences and related glossary/migration entries, plus concise verification and revision references.

A matching record may satisfy later checks only after its evidence and concept/use/scope match are verified. Keep this working record out of reader-facing prose unless requested.
