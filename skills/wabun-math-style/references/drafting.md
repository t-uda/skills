# Japanese mathematical drafting preferences

Apply to new prose, translation and requested stylistic rewrites. These preferences create no ADVISORY, MINOR or BLOCKING finding merely from a word used in existing clear prose.

- Name the actual order or enumeration instead of an imprecise `順位` label. Do not invent a total order or enumeration absent from the argument.
- Do not mechanically expand conventions and auxiliary technical steps from formalization into prose. Lean and similar systems require explicit scaffolding that mathematical readers can often supply. Write `空和は0である`, or `空和は0だから` in an argument, without unnecessary provenance or definition procedure. Retain nonstandard conventions, consequential boundary conditions, the fact and reasoning needed in the empty case, and every necessary argument.
- Prefer `構成する` for an explicit operation producing an object: `その順に $0,1,\ldots,n-1$ を割り当て，次数条件を満たす全単射を構成する`. When the operation is clear, `得る` or `とおく` remains acceptable.

Clear uses of `順位`, `規約`, `得る` or `とおく` are not word-based defects. Ambiguous objects, missing operations and missing arguments need substantive repairs under their owning skills.
